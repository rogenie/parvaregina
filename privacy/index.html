Privacy

Privacy & cookies

Effective October 29, 2026. In short: we collect your email only if you give it to us, we don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Who we are

Parva Regina (parvaregina.com) is published by 9nth Dimension Inc., based in California. “We” and “us” in this policy mean 9nth Dimension Inc.

What we collect

WhatWhere it comes fromWhy we use it
Your email address, and which download you asked forYou, when you sign up for a free guideTo send the download and our weekly longevity email
Email activity, such as whether an email was opened or a link clickedOur email service, KitTo see which emails are useful and keep the list healthy
Anonymous visit counts: page viewed, referring site, browser type, screen size and countryYour browser, through GoatCounterTo see which articles people read

We don’t ask for your name, address, phone number, health conditions or payment details, and we don’t create profiles of visitors. The topic of a guide you download (for example, a meal plan) tells us your interest, not anything about your health, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Cookies, pixels and tracking

  • No cookies. Parva Regina doesn’t set cookies.
  • No advertising or social media pixels. We don’t use the Meta (Facebook) pixel, TikTok pixel, Google Analytics, Google Ads tags, session recording or any similar tool.
  • Privacy-friendly visit counts. GoatCounter counts page views without cookies, doesn’t store your IP address, and doesn’t track you across other websites.
  • Signup forms load nothing until you use them. Our forms are plain forms on our own pages. Your email is sent to Kit only when you press the button.
  • Fonts and images are served from our own site.

If we ever add a tool that sets cookies or tracks visitors for advertising, we’ll update this page first and ask for your consent before it runs.

Who helps us (service providers)

These companies process data only to provide their service to us, under contracts or terms that restrict their use of it:

  • Kit (Kit.com): sends our emails and stores the subscriber list.
  • GoatCounter: anonymous visit counts.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

We don’t sell or share personal information

We have not sold or “shared” (for cross-context behavioral advertising) personal information in the past 12 months, and we don’t do so now. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Because we don’t track visitors across sites or sell or share personal information, your browser’s Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal doesn’t change anything on our site: you already get the same treatment those signals ask for. We treat both signals as a valid opt-out request.

How long we keep data

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. After you unsubscribe, Kit may keep your address on a suppression list so you aren’t emailed again. Visit counts are kept in aggregate.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe with one click from the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information by replying to any of our emails with your request.

Your California privacy rights

If you live in California, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use and disclose (described above), and get a copy of yours
  • Delete personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we don’t sell or share it)
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information (we don’t collect it)
  • Not be discriminated against for using these rights

To make a request, reply to any of our emails, or email us using the address in our email footer. We’ll confirm your request by matching the email address you used, and respond within 45 days. You can use an authorized agent; we may ask them for proof that you authorized them. Under California’s “Shine the Light” law, you may also ask whether we disclosed personal information to third parties for their direct marketing; we don’t.

Security

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is little to protect.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, such as adding cookies or advertising tools, we’ll tell subscribers by email before it takes effect.
