Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026.

What we collect

Parva Regina has no accounts or comments and doesn’t set cookies. We don’t sell or share personal information.

Visit counts

We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It records which page was viewed, the referring site, and general browser, screen size and country information. It uses no cookies, doesn’t track you across other sites, and doesn’t store your IP address. We use these totals only to see which articles are read.

Newsletter

If you join the newsletter, your email address is collected and stored by Kit, the service that sends it, under its own privacy policy. You can unsubscribe from any email.

Services that load with the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web server, these services receive your IP address and basic browser information when you load a page, under their own privacy policies.

Changes

If we add advertising, affiliate links or other analytics, this page will be updated first and will say what is collected and why.
