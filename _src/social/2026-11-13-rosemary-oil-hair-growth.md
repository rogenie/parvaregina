Does rosemary oil really grow hair as well as minoxidil?

1. The claim comes from one 2015 trial of 100 men aged 18-49: after 6 months, hair counts rose in both the rosemary oil and 2% minoxidil groups, with no difference between them. (Evidence: human trial)
2. There was no placebo group, and no women were studied, so it can't show how much rosemary itself did, or whether it helps women. (Evidence: human trial)
3. Rosemary oil can irritate skin and trigger skin allergy in some people. Lab and mouse results on rosemary extract aren't proof for people. (Evidence: review)
