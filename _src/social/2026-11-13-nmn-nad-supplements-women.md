NMN and NR promise to raise NAD+. Do they actually do anything for your health?

1. Yes, they raise NAD+ in blood. But most human trials are small and short, and measurable health benefits have been inconsistent. (Evidence: review)
2. In 25 postmenopausal women with prediabetes, 10 weeks of NMN improved muscle insulin sensitivity, while body fat, blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol didn't change. (Evidence: human trial)
3. In 2025 the FDA decided NMN can be sold as a supplement. That was a legal decision, not a review of safety or effectiveness. (Evidence: official source)
