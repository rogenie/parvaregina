Does urolithin A actually do anything for muscle and mitochondria?

1. In a 4-month trial of 66 adults aged 65 to 90 (about 76% women), 1,000 mg a day didn't improve walking distance more than placebo. Muscle endurance improved at 2 months, but the placebo group caught up by 4 months. (Evidence: human trial)
2. In a 4-month trial of 88 adults aged about 40 to 65 (about 64% women), hamstring strength rose, but the main outcome, cycling power, didn't beat placebo. (Evidence: human trial)
3. The lifespan results come from worms, and a 2026 review of 5 small trials rated the evidence low certainty. (Evidence: review)
