Menopause isn't a slow fade. A study of 300 million lab tests from over a million women found an abrupt shift at the final period.

1. Bone, liver and blood-fat markers changed suddenly around the last period. [Human observational]
2. Key hormones started shifting 12 to 15 years earlier, often in the late 30s.
3. It shows when things change, not what to do about it. No supplement needed.
