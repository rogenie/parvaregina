Is your hair thinning, shedding or falling out in patches, and what's actually behind it?

1. Gradual thinning on top with a widening part is usually female pattern hair loss. A 2018 review reports about 25% of women show it by age 49. (Evidence: review)
2. Sudden all-over shedding often follows a stressor like illness, surgery or childbirth by about 3 months, and is usually temporary. (Evidence: review)
3. A receding hairline band, eyebrow loss, or an itchy or sore scalp can signal scarring hair loss, which is worth getting checked early by a board-certified dermatologist. (Evidence: review)
