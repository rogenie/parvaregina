Does lifting weights actually help your bones around menopause?

1. In an 8-month trial of 101 postmenopausal women with low bone mass (average age 65), supervised heavy lifting plus jumping raised spine bone density 2.9%, while a low-intensity home program lost 1.2%. (Evidence: human trial)
2. The program was supervised, started with technique practice and built up gradually. One minor back spasm was reported. (Evidence: human trial)
3. A 2026 pilot in 40 women aged 45 to 60 found spine density and lean mass higher after 9 months, but it was a feasibility study, not proof. (Evidence: human trial)
