Do LED masks actually smooth wrinkles after 40?

1. The popular mask studies were small (20 and 33 people), short, and had no comparison group. Most were paid for by the brands. (Evidence: company-funded human studies, no control group)
2. "FDA cleared" isn't "proven to work." One mask cleared in 2026 needed no clinical testing, just a comparison with older devices and lab safety tests.
3. Use the eye protection that comes with the mask. FDA says these devices can pose a risk to the eyes.
