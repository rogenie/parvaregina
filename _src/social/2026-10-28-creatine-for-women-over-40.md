Everyone says creatine is the midlife woman's supplement. What did the trials in women actually find?

1. Bone: a 2-year trial in 237 postmenopausal women found no bone-density benefit. [Human trial]
2. Strength: everyone who strength-trained got stronger. Creatine added little, mostly upper body. [Review, low certainty]
3. Brain fog: one 8-week study of 36 women. Interesting, far too early to count on. [Human trial, small]
