What is VO2 max, and can women raise it in midlife?

1. In a study of 122,007 adults (about 41% women), the least-fit group had about 5 times the death risk of the fittest. That's an association, not proof of cause. (Evidence: human observational)
2. In a 6-month trial of 464 postmenopausal women, even about 72 minutes a week of moderate exercise raised fitness, and more exercise raised it more. (Evidence: human trial)
3. Interval training raised VO2 max in trials of midlife and older women, but steady exercise worked too. Watch estimates are best for tracking your own trend. (Evidence: human trials)
